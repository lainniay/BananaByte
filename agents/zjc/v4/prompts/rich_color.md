Generate a color-only richness candidate for the image. Focus only on making the natural colors richer, more complete, and more locally consistent. Improve missing, faded, weak, or biased chroma while keeping the recovery restrained and plausible. Do not try to enhance sharpness, texture, or structure. Return image with improved color only. Preserve the exact composition, object shapes, edges, texture positions, brightness structure, and scene content. Do not crop, rotate, add objects, remove objects, repaint details, sharpen, blur, or change lighting/contrast aggressively.
