# Prompt Policy

The generated `nano_banana_prompt` is not free-form. It must be a concrete editing instruction that satisfies all policy requirements below.

## Required Clauses

The `nano_banana_prompt` must explicitly include all of the following ideas:

1. **Input roles**
   - The first image is the source low-resolution image.
   - The second image is a same-size, pixel-aligned text-region mask.
   - White mask regions are likely text or text-like regions.

2. **Text-mask behavior**
   - In white mask regions, preserve the existing stroke layout, spacing, orientation, and character-like shapes.
   - Do not invent, rewrite, translate, complete, or replace unreadable text.
   - Do not turn blurry text-like strokes into guessed sharp characters.
   - Prefer conservative deblurring and gentle cleanup in white mask regions.
   - It is acceptable for masked text-like regions to remain slightly soft if that avoids hallucinated characters.

3. **Non-text behavior**
   - Outside the white mask regions, improve natural image clarity, edges, and texture.
   - Do not introduce new objects, decorative patterns, symbols, or text.

4. **Global preservation**
   - Preserve the exact framing, field of view, perspective, object layout, colors, lighting, and composition.
   - The result must look like the same image at higher resolution, not a new image inspired by it.

5. **Conservative priority**
   - Faithfulness is more important than sharpness in text-like regions.
   - If a detail is not visible in the source image, do not fabricate it.

## Forbidden Prompt Tendencies

The `nano_banana_prompt` must not ask Nano Banana to:

- make text readable by guessing characters;
- restore, translate, complete, or correct text content;
- sharpen all edges uniformly without respecting the mask;
- beautify the image by adding new textures or design elements;
- reinterpret the scene.

## Prompt Self-Check

Before returning JSON, verify that the `nano_banana_prompt` answers these questions:

- Does it clearly say that white mask regions require conservative text preservation?
- Does it explicitly forbid inventing or completing unreadable characters?
- Does it allow normal SR improvement outside the mask?
- Does it preserve framing and composition?
